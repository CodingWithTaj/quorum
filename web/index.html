    We tried to break it, 10,000 times.

    Clicking around shows Raft working. It doesn't prove it. So the same code runs inside a deterministic simulator that throws randomized disasters at it: servers crashing and bouncing back within milliseconds, the network splitting and healing, messages lost, duplicated and reordered, all while clients keep writing.

    After every single event, a checker verifies the four safety guarantees from the Raft paper. Across 10,000 scenarios, 35 hours of simulated time and 23 million messages, none has ever been violated.

    A checker that never fails could just be broken itself. So below you can switch on one of four classic Raft bugs, and watch it get caught and replayed.
